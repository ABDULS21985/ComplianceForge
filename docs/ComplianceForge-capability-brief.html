ComplianceForge

Enterprise Governance, Risk & Compliance Platform

One Platform. Every Obligation. Clear Accountability.

ComplianceForge is a connected GRC platform designed for organisations managing overlapping standards and regulation across the UK and Europe. It gives compliance, risk, audit, privacy and leadership teams one operating environment—from initial framework selection through evidence, remediation and board reporting.

A unified data model links obligations to controls, risks, policies, owners, evidence, findings and actions. Instead of rebuilding the same assurance story for every framework, teams can reuse common controls, coordinate approvals and surface priorities in role-based dashboards.

What the foundation establishes

  • A shared information model connects frameworks, controls, implementations, evidence, risks, policies, audits, incidents, vendors and accountable owners—supporting a traceable assurance story.
  • Dedicated internal, vendor and board experiences bring operational teams and invited stakeholders into focused workflows while preserving the context leadership needs for oversight.

Why this matters beyond a control register

The same control can satisfy multiple obligations, support several audits and reduce repeated evidence requests. ComplianceForge is designed to preserve those relationships, so a change in control status, evidence currency or risk treatment can be understood across the wider compliance landscape.

That connected model creates the foundation for workflow automation, continuous monitoring, AI-assisted remediation and executive analytics—turning compliance from a periodic reporting exercise into an operating discipline with visible ownership and follow-through.
